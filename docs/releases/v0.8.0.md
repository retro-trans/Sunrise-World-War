English fan translation of **Sunrise World War from Sunrise Eiyuutan** for PlayStation 2. This first private test release is based on the v0.3.20 test build.

### Apply

Use Delta Patcher or xdelta3 with an unpacked disc image from your own Japanese copy.

| Source image | Patch |
|---|---|
| Original Japanese release, SLPS-25270 | `SWW-English-v0.8.0.xdelta` |

```powershell
xdelta3 -d -s "Sunrise World War (Japan).iso" SWW-English-v0.8.0.xdelta "Sunrise World War EN v0.8.0.iso"
```

Extract CHD inputs to an ISO or BIN before patching. Keep checksum verification enabled. Boot the patched image fresh and load a normal memory-card save. Older save states can restore the previous executable and graphics.

### What’s included

- **Story and game text.** Dialogue, briefings, profiles, unit and pilot names, equipment and help text. The build applies 9,815 translated rows across 43 text tables, plus executable string edits.
- **Interface artwork.** 269 localized sprites, including 42 direct ImageGen edits. The Info screens and equipment, targeting, and ship badges are among the latest updates.
- **Opening song.** “Destination” has 19 English subtitle cues timed with Whisper and reviewed against the Japanese transcription. The Japanese lines include romaji above English. The game draws both lines during playback.

### Validation and testing status

The xdelta patch was decoded and checked against the complete v0.3.20 image. The subtitle renderer was checked at cue boundaries and on decoded opening frames. Full route playtesting and audits of some tactical map labels, movie textures, and model textures are pending.
